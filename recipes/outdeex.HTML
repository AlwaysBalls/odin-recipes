FRIED POTATOES

this is patrick's fresh fried potatoes, very hot and crunchy

ingredients

  • yam
  • kings oil
  • salt
  • chilli

steps

  1. peal and wash ya potatoes
  2. add a little salt to it before frying
  3. set your oilon fire to get hot
  4. fry your potatoes on low heat for 10 mins
  5. sprinkle your chilli on it after frying
